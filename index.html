FAQ

Good questions.

How do I book a consultation?
Use the booking form below: choose an office, a day and a time. We will confirm by phone or email. You can also call Pretoria on 012 880 3156 or Polokwane on 015 880 1282.
Which office should I choose?
Whichever is closer to you. We have offices in Brooklyn, Pretoria and in Polokwane, and the same team serves both.
What happens after I submit a form?
Your request reaches our team, and we contact you to confirm. Submitting a form does not create an attorney–client relationship until we confirm it.
My matter is urgent. What should I do?
Please call us directly rather than waiting for a booking: Pretoria 012 880 3156, Polokwane 015 880 1282, or cell 079 463 9106.
